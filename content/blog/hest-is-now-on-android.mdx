We've been hearing you, and today we're excited to announce that **Hest is officially available on Android**. 🎉

Since launching on iOS, thousands of households have used Hest to bring order to the chaos of everyday life. Shared calendars, task assignments, meal planning, recipe libraries, and household records are all available in one private, beautifully designed app. Now, every member of your household can join in, regardless of what phone they carry.

## Why Android?

From day one, our most-requested feature wasn't a new screen or a fancy integration. It was **"When is Hest coming to Android?"**

We get it. Households aren't single-platform. You might be on an iPhone while your partner is on a Samsung Galaxy. Your college-age kid might have a Pixel. The reality of modern households is that people use different devices, and a household app only works when *everyone* can use it.

Today, that barrier is gone.

## Everything You Love, Now on Android

The Android version of Hest isn't a stripped-down port. It's the **full Hest experience**, built on the same codebase with the same features, real-time sync, and attention to detail.

Here's what you get on day one:

### 📅 Shared Calendar with External Sync

Import your Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook events and see your entire household's schedule in one place. No more double-booking, no more "I didn't know you had plans."

### ✅ Task Management with Assignments

Create tasks, assign them to household members, set due dates, and get push notifications when things are created or completed. Everyone stays accountable.

### 🍽️ Meal Planning & Recipes

Plan your weekly meals, assign cooks, and link recipes from your personal recipe library. Go from "what's for dinner?" to a full plan in minutes.

### 📁 Household Records

Store warranties, insurance details, account numbers, and important documents in one secure, shared location. No more digging through email threads or kitchen drawers.

## Seamless and Instant Cross-Platform Sync

The magic of Hest is that it doesn't matter who's on iOS and who's on Android. Everything syncs in real time. When someone adds an event, assigns a task, or updates the meal plan, every household member sees it instantly regardless of their device.

Your household data isn't locked to a platform. It's locked to your household.

## Free to Download, Free to Start

Just like on iOS, Hest on Android is **free to download** with a generous free tier. You get full access to shared calendars, task management, meal planning, recipes, and household records. There are no ads and no data selling, just a clean, private experience for your household.

An optional premium subscription unlocks features for larger households, but most families will find everything they need in the free tier.

## How to Get Started

Getting your household on Hest takes less than two minutes:

1. **Download Hest** from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rollingtrunk.hest&referrer=utm_source%3Dhest_website%26utm_medium%3Dweb%26utm_campaign%3DHest%2520Website) or the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hest-household-manager/id6759582460)
2. **Create your household** and invite your members
3. **Start organizing:** Add your first event, create a task, or plan this week's meals

It's that simple.
