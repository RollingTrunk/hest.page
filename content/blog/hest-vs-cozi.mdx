Managing a household takes coordination. Between shared calendars, chore lists, meal planning, and keeping track of important documents, most families end up juggling a patchwork of apps that don't talk to each other.

Two apps aiming to solve this are **Hest** and **Cozi**. Both are built for households — but they take very different approaches. Here's how they compare.

## At a Glance

| Feature | Hest | Cozi |
|---|---|---|
| **Shared Calendar** | <Yes>With external sync (Google, Apple, Outlook)</Yes> | <Yes>Built-in family calendar</Yes> |
| **Task Management** | <Yes>Assignable tasks with categories & due dates</Yes> | <Yes>Basic to-do lists</Yes> |
| **Meal Planning** | <Yes>Weekly planner with recipe linking</Yes> | <Yes>Meal planning with recipe box</Yes> |
| **Recipe Management** | <Yes>Full recipe CRUD with meal plan integration</Yes> | <Yes>Recipe box with web import</Yes> |
| **Household Records** | <Yes>Document & record tracking</Yes> | <No>Not available</No> |
| **Push Notifications** | <Yes>Real-time for all events</Yes> | <Yes>Daily agenda notifications</Yes> |
| **External Calendar Sync** | <Yes>Google, Apple, Outlook</Yes> | <No>Manual entry only</No> |
| **Price** | Free tier + optional premium | Free with ads, Gold upgrade |
| **Platforms** | iOS (Android coming soon) | iOS, Android, Web |

## Calendar

Both apps offer shared family calendars, but Hest takes a different approach by **syncing with your existing calendars**. Instead of manually re-entering events, Hest imports from Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook — so you see everything in one place without duplicating effort.

Cozi's calendar is standalone. It works well for family-specific events, but if your schedule lives in Google or Outlook, you'll need to manage it in two places.

## Tasks & Chores

Cozi provides simple shared to-do lists. They're functional for grocery runs and quick reminders.

Hest goes further with **assignable tasks** — you can assign a task to a specific household member, set a due date, categorize it, and everyone gets push notifications when tasks are created or updated. This makes it better suited for delegating chores and keeping everyone accountable.

## Meal Planning

Both apps support meal planning, but the integration level differs. In Hest, meal plans connect directly to your **recipe library** — you can link a saved recipe to any meal, assign a cook, and see the full week at a glance.

Cozi's meal planner and recipe box are separate features that don't deeply integrate with each other.

## Records & Documents

This is a feature unique to Hest. The **Household Records** module lets you store and organize important information — think warranties, insurance details, account numbers, or any document your household needs to access.

Cozi doesn't offer anything comparable.

## Privacy

Hest is built with a **privacy-first** approach. Your data is encrypted, and personal information is never sold or shared with third parties.

Cozi's free tier is ad-supported, which means some level of data usage for advertising purposes.

## The Bottom Line

**Choose Cozi if** you want a well-established, multi-platform family organizer with a simple feature set and don't need calendar sync or document tracking.

**Choose Hest if** you want a modern, all-in-one household hub that syncs with your existing calendars, lets you assign tasks to family members, and keeps important records in one place — all without ads.
