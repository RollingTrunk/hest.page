# CRO Checklist

## Above the Fold
- [ ] **Value Proposition**: A headline that clearly states what you do and who it's for.
- [ ] **Sub-headline**: Explains *how* you do it or the primary benefit.
- [ ] **Primary CTA**: A single, high-contrast button telling them what to do (e.g., "Start Free Trial").
- [ ] **Hero Visual**: An image or video showing the product in action.

## Friction Removal
- [ ] **Navigation**: Remove header links on landing pages to keep users focused on the CTA.
- [ ] **Forms**: Reduce fields to the absolute minimum (email only if possible).
- [ ] **Speed**: Ensure the page loads in under 2 seconds.

## Trust Builders
- [ ] **Social Proof**: Testimonials, reviews, or "Trusted by" logos.
- [ ] **Risk Reversal**: "No credit card required" or "30-day money-back guarantee".
- [ ] **FAQ**: Address common objections directly on the page.
