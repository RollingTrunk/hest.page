# Copywriting Guidelines

## The Framework
1. **Who is it for?** (Audience)
2. **What is the problem?** (Pain)
3. **How does this solve it?** (Solution)
4. **Why trust us?** (Proof)
5. **What to do next?** (Action)

## Best Practices
- **Clarity over Cleverness**: If they don't understand it, they won't buy it.
- **You-Focused**: Use "You" 2x more than "We".
- **Specifics Sell**: "Save 2 hours/day" is better than "Save time".
- **Headlines**: Should stop the scroll. Use curiosity, benefit, or controversy.

## Common Formats
- **PAS**: Pain, Agitate, Solution.
- **AIDA**: Attention, Interest, Desire, Action.
- **Before-After-Bridge**: Show life before, life after, and your product as the bridge.
