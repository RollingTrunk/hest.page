# Growth Strategies

## Engineering as Marketing
- **Free Tools**: Build calculators, generators, or analyzers that solve a small, specific problem for your target audience.
- **Open Source**: Release a useful component or library to build developer goodwill.

## Content Marketing
- **Comparison Posts**: "Best X for Y", "Alternates to [Competitor]".
- **Case Studies**: Deep dives into how a customer solved a specific problem using your product.
- **Templates**: specific templates for notion/excel/etc that relate to the problem.

## Community & Social
- **Niche Communities**: Participate in subreddits, IndieHackers, or specialized Discords.
- **#BuildingInPublic**: Share your journey, wins, and losses on Twitter/LinkedIn.

## Partnerships
- **Integrations**: Partner with complementary tools to cross-promote.
- **Affiliates**: Incentivize users to refer others.
